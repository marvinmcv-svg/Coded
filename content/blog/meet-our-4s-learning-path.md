In this blog post, we’ll explore how our four-steps learning approach, combining experts delivered, project-based learning (PBL), live workshops at the parks, and extended coding lessons, not only empowers students with creativity, authentic experiences, student agency and competition, but also embraces inclusion and differentiation throughout the entire Coded experience.

In today’s landscape, creativity isn’t merely a desirable trait; it is absolutely indispensable. At Coded, we firmly believe that **innovation** is the fundamental skill that distinguishes our students. Our meticulously crafted four-step journey is geared toward fostering creativity in every single one of our participants.

We truly believe in learning by doing. That’s why we prioritize **authentic experiences** through a project-based learning approach. We place students in real-world scenarios, connecting them with experts who share their knowledge on the skills they’ll need to face in tech careers. This hands-on engagement is about nurturing creativity by encouraging students to develop innovative projects.

Creativity flourishes when students have the freedom to explore and experiment. Our journey emphasizes **student agency,** enabling them to take charge of their education. Throughout our programs, they become active participants that make decisions, set goals, and think creatively to solve complex problems. This autonomy ignites their imaginative spark and instills a sense of ownership over their creative endeavors.

Healthy **competition** sparks creativity. Our students engage in friendly competition, pushing one another to excel and think outside the box. Whether it’s project challenges or collaborative events at our live workshops, competition encourages them to innovate and explore new avenues, reinforcing the importance of creativity in their tech journey.

Creativity thrives when individuals are allowed to follow their unique passions and interests. Our four-step journey accommodates this diversity through **differentiation**. While our STEAM curricular approach remains comprehensive, our variety of hand-on experiences and extended coding lessons, enable students to delve deeper into specialized areas of interest, fostering their creative exploration in their chosen domains.

Coded’s four-step journey is not just about acquiring technical skills; it’s about cultivating creativity that empowers our students to become tech leaders and innovators. **Through authentic experiences, student agency, healthy competition, and differentiation, we create an environment where creativity is not only encouraged but celebrated.**

We are dedicated to empowering the next generation of tech leaders who will not only shape the future of technology but also leave a lasting impact on the world through their imaginative and innovative proposals.
